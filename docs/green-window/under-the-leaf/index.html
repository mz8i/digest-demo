Look beneath before reaching for a spray

Three clues hidden under a leaf

A curled or spotted leaf rarely tells the whole story from above. Turn it over and look for insects, webbing, eggs, or clean tissue; the underside helps distinguish a visitor from watering stress or old damage.

  • Check several leaves, including one that still looks healthy.
  • Photograph the pattern before removing damaged growth.
  • Isolate a badly affected pot while you identify the cause.
