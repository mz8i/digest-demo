Notes from a small patch

Leave a few hollow stems standing

The useful mess inside a winter pot

Dry stems catch frost beautifully, but they also offer narrow winter shelter for small insects. Keep the strongest ones until warmer weather, then cut them into short lengths and leave them loosely near the soil.

Tidying later can be a form of making room now.
