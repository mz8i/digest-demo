Re-wet a dry pot slowly

Compost that has stopped drinking

Very dry compost can shrink from the pot and let water rush down the gap. Add a small amount, wait, and repeat until the surface begins absorbing again; one dramatic soaking often passes the roots entirely.

If water still escapes immediately, stand the pot in a shallow tray for twenty minutes. Remove it afterwards so the roots are not left sitting without air.
