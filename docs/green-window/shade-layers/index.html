Notes from a small patch

Build shade in layers

A balcony can have a woodland edge

A tall grass, a broad-leaved perennial, and a low trailing plant can recreate the useful layers of a woodland edge in three containers. Each catches light differently and shelters the soil beneath it.

Choose plants for the light you actually have, not for the scene you want to imitate. The woodland idea is about structure and shelter; the species can be humble and locally suited.
