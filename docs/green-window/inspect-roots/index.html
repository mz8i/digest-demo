A crowded pot is not always an urgent pot

Before you repot, inspect the roots

Roots circling the outer compost show that a plant has explored its container, not necessarily that it is suffering. Repot when water runs straight through, growth stalls, or the plant becomes unstable—not simply because roots are visible.

  • Move up only one container size at a time.
  • Keep the plant at the same soil depth.
  • Water once, then let the fresh compost settle naturally.
