One useful money note

Fewer transfers can make a plan clearer

The hidden cost of moving money too often

Constantly shifting small amounts between accounts makes it hard to understand what the plan expected. Choose a regular transfer rhythm, then record exceptions briefly instead of disguising them as routine.

Clarity matters more than perfect allocation. A plan you can reconstruct three weeks later will teach you more than one that looked precise only on the day you made it.
