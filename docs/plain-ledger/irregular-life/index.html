Make room for the unsurprising surprise

A budget category for irregular life

Shoes wear out, guests visit, and small appliances fail. The date is uncertain, but the category is not. A modest line for irregular life keeps these ordinary events from masquerading as emergencies.

Begin with the last six months rather than an ideal month. Circle expenses that were unusual in timing but normal in kind, then use their average as a calm first estimate.
