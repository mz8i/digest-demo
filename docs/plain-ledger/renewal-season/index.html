Gather annual decisions into one month

Try a renewal season instead of twelve reminders

Scattered renewals create repeated low-grade admin. Where providers allow it, moving several reviews into one chosen month can turn a year of interruptions into a short, predictable household routine.

  • List renewal dates before changing any of them.
  • Avoid bunching payments if that would strain cash flow.
  • Keep essential cover active while comparing options.
