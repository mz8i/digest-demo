A note for lending money to someone you love

A short written note can protect a relationship from two sincere but different memories. Record whether the money is a gift or loan, the amount, and what should happen if repayment becomes difficult.

  • Use ordinary language rather than pretending to be a bank.
  • Do not lend money you need for your own essentials.
  • Agree how either person can raise a problem early.
