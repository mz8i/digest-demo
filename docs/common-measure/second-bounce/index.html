Materials return energy differently

The second bounce tells the story

Drop equal balls from the same height onto several surfaces and compare the next peak. The rebound shows how much energy the ball and floor stored, returned, or lost as heat and sound.

  • Use the same release height.
  • Film from the side.
  • Compare several bounces, not one.
