Design for the wet, hurried minute

Why public chargers need boring instructions

A street charger is often used in rain, glare, and a hurry. One durable sequence printed beside the connector outperforms a polished help page available only after something fails.

  • Show the order of operations.
  • Name the visible success state.
  • Provide a non-app fallback.
