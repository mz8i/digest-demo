A nudge at apogee

The small correction that saves a satellite

Operators often wait for the far point of an orbit to make a tiny, efficient correction. The timing turns a modest burn into a large change elsewhere, a reminder that orbital work is as much about patience as power.

The burn may last only moments, but teams rehearse its direction, duration, and fallback plan for days. The smallest commands often receive the longest preparation because there is no easy way to reach out and try again.
