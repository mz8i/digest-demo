The broth hiding in your freezer

Keep clean mushroom stems, herb stalks, and the pale parts of spring onions in one freezer bag. When it is full, simmer the contents for forty minutes and strain; longer cooking can make the vegetables taste tired.

Avoid strongly bitter scraps and anything already beginning to spoil. Stock is generous with odd shapes, not with tired flavour, and the freezer cannot improve an ingredient that was unpleasant when it went in.
