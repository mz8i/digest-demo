Slow leeks with mustard crumbs

Make leeks the centre of the plate

Split leeks lengthwise, wash between the layers, and cook them cut-side down until deeply coloured. Add a splash of water, cover until soft, then scatter with mustardy breadcrumbs for a supper that needs little beside it.
