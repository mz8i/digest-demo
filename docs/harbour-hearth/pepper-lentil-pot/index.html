From the kitchen table

Roasted pepper and lentil pot

A deep-red soup for windy evenings

Roast peppers until their edges darken, then simmer them with red lentils, garlic, and a pinch of smoked spice. Blend only half the pot so it stays substantial, and finish with lemon just before serving.

  • For a softer bowl, add a spoonful of yoghurt.
  • For more texture, keep a quarter of the roasted pepper aside until the end.
  • For tomorrow, loosen the soup with water before reheating.
