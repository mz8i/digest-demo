Designing a place to look away

Why the quiet corner wins

A workspace feels calmer when it includes somewhere deliberately unimportant. A patch of wall, a low shelf, or a distant lamp gives the eyes a neutral destination between decisions, instead of surrounding the person with competing prompts.

The useful test is not whether that corner looks beautiful in a still image. It is whether a person naturally rests their gaze there after finishing a task, without being told that this is what the space is for.
