Reach should feel smaller than the room

The two-second reach test

We timed common seated gestures and found that the comfortable ones finished before the shoulder joined in. Put frequent actions inside that easy envelope; reserve the wider sweep for moments that deserve ceremony.

  • Keep repeated selections near the resting hand.
  • Let infrequent tools come closer when summoned.
  • Test both hands even when the interaction seems symmetrical.
