What the edge of the frame is doing

A bright scrap, half a face, or a line leaving the corner can pull attention away from the subject. Scan the border once before pressing the shutter.

  • Check all four corners.
  • Notice lines that leave the frame.
  • Decide whether partial objects feel intentional.
