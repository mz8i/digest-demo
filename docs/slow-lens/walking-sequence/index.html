Let distance become the edit

A sequence made from walking speed

Photograph a subject once from far away, once as it becomes recognisable, and once when it fills the frame. The three pictures form a natural approach without changing lenses.

Keep your height and direction consistent. The sequence becomes stronger when distance is the only obvious variable.
