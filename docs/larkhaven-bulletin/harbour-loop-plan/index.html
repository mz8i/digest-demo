The harbour loop keeps its late connection

What changed in the harbour bus plan

Transport planners have revised the proposed harbour bus timetable after evening workers raised concerns about the last connection. The final weekday departure will remain at 23:10, while two lightly used afternoon journeys will be combined.

  • The new timetable begins on the first Monday of next month.
  • Weekend service is unchanged.
  • Printed schedules will appear at stops one week before the switch.
