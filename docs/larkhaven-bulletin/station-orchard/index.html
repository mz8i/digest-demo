Around the city today

Station volunteers plant twelve fruit trees

A tiny orchard behind Platform Four

A narrow strip behind Platform Four now holds twelve young pear and plum trees, planted by station staff and the Northbank allotment group. The fruit will be free to pick once the trees are established, though that is likely several seasons away.

For now, the harvest is shade and a better walk to the bicycle racks.
