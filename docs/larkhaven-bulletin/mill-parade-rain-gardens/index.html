Kerbside beds will hold back heavy rain

Rain gardens planned for Mill Parade

Four planted beds are planned for the lower end of Mill Parade, where short storms regularly overwhelm the street drains. Gaps in the kerb will guide water into deep soil before it reaches the junction.

  • Existing loading spaces remain in place.
  • The beds use salt-tolerant grasses and low shrubs.
  • Construction is scheduled outside the winter market period.
