The Lantern Room opens with forty seats

A cinema returns to Foundry Lane

An empty furniture showroom on Foundry Lane has reopened as the Lantern Room, a forty-seat neighbourhood cinema. Its first programme pairs recent short films with restored travel pictures found in a cupboard at the old ferry office.
