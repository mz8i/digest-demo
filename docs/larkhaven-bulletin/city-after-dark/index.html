Around the city today

A new gallery tells the city after dark

The museum labels written by night workers

The City Rooms has invited bakers, cleaners, ferry crews, and hospital porters to write the labels for its next gallery. Ordinary objects—a thermos, a key ring, a reflective jacket—anchor short accounts of Larkhaven between midnight and dawn.

The city does not close; it changes hands.
