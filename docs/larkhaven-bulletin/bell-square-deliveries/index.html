Delivery hours shift around Bell Square

Bell Square gets a quieter morning

A six-week trial will move most shop deliveries to the lane behind Bell Square before the morning foot traffic begins. The city says the change should leave the central paving clearer for market stalls and people crossing to the tram stop.

Three corner shops asked for a later window on Mondays, when their larger orders arrive. The published trial includes that exception and a review meeting after the first fortnight.
