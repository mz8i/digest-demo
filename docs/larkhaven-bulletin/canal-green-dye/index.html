Around the city today

A harmless dye maps a hidden drain

Why the canal turned green overnight

The bright green colour near Weaver Lock came from a tracing dye used to find where rainwater enters an older section of drain. The water team says the colour is harmless and should fade completely by tomorrow morning.

Crews found the suspected connection beneath a disused loading yard. Repair work will take place from the yard, so the towpath is expected to remain open.
