Students restore the west-pier tide gauge

The old tide gauge will measure again

Engineering students from Larkhaven College have rebuilt the simple float mechanism inside the west-pier tide gauge. Its painted scale will once again show the water level through the glass door, alongside a small digital sensor used for comparison.

The public display is deliberately mechanical: a wire, a wheel, and a counterweight make the changing tide visible without a screen. Readings will also be archived for classroom projects.
